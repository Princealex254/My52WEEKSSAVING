User Guide & FAQ

1. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your main control center. Here you can see your Current Savings Goal for the active week, your total progress, and key statistics.

  • Weekly Increase: The amount added to your target each week (e.g., Week 1 = 50, Week 2 = 100).
  • Target Total: The total amount you will have saved by Week 52.
  • Rollover: Any extra amount you saved in previous weeks carries over to help reduce the current week's target.

2. How to Save

Use the green Action Card at the top of the dashboard to record your savings.

  • Check the Net Needed amount for the current week.
  • Enter the amount you have saved in the input box.
  • Click Save Transaction.
  • You can also use the "Quick Actions" buttons (+ Exact, + Half) for faster entry.

3. Weekly Tracker & Settings

Navigate to the Weekly Tracker tab to see a full list of all 52 weeks, check which are paid, and download a PDF report.

In Challenge Settings, you can adjust your Weekly Increment or Start Date. Changing these will recalculate your targets for the entire year.
